## The short version

This site has no accounts, no advertising, and no cross-site tracking.
Most of it is static pages with running demos. What does get processed:
server logs when pages are delivered, cookieless usage statistics, error
reports when something breaks, an email address if you sign up for the
Monday digest, and whatever you write us through the contact form. One
demo sets one cookie, and it says so right next to the button. The
details, legal bases and your rights follow below.

## Legal bases

Where this policy names a legal basis, it refers to Art. 6 (1) GDPR:

- **(a) consent** — you actively agreed, and can withdraw at any time;
- **(b) contract** — processing is needed to do the thing you asked for;
- **(f) legitimate interest** — running this site securely and reliably.

For storing or reading information on your device (cookies, local
storage), § 25 TDDDG applies; this site only uses storage that is
strictly necessary for a function you use (§ 25 (2) TDDDG), so no
consent banner is required.

## Hosting

This site is hosted by **Vercel Inc.**, 650 California St, San
Francisco, CA 94108, USA. When you open a page, Vercel processes the
technical data every web server needs: your IP address, the requested
URL, date and time, transferred data volume, referrer, and user-agent.
This happens in server logs, on the legal basis of our legitimate
interest (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR) in delivering the site securely.

place ([vercel.com/legal/dpa](https://vercel.com/legal/dpa)).

## Error monitoring

When something on the site crashes, an error report is sent to
**Sentry** (Functional Software, Inc., 45 Fremont Street, San
Francisco, CA 94105, USA), containing the technical context of the
error — which may include your IP address, user-agent, and the state of
the page at the time. Our Sentry project uses EU data residency, so
error data is processed within the European Union; a data processing
agreement is in place. Legal basis: legitimate interest
(Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR) in finding and fixing defects. IP addresses are
not used to identify visitors, and error events are retained for 90
days.

## Usage statistics

This site uses **Vercel Web Analytics**, which counts page views and
interactions without cookies and without persistent identifiers:
visitors are distinguished by a short-lived hash that cannot be traced
across sites or days. No advertising IDs, no cross-site profiles. Legal
basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR) in knowing which pages
are read.

## Cookies and local storage

This site sets exactly one cookie: `playground-presses`, an `httpOnly`
cookie holding the press count of the Server Actions demo — it exists
so the demo can prove a point about cookies your JavaScript cannot
read, and it is functional storage under § 25 (2) TDDDG. It expires
after one year and identifies nothing but a number.

Your theme choice (light, dark, or system) is kept in your browser's
local storage, never sent to us. Fonts are self-hosted; no requests go
to font CDNs.

## The Monday digest

If you sign up for the weekly email, we process your email address —
nothing else — to send you the digest. Signup is double opt-in: you get
a confirmation email, and only a confirmed address is ever written to
the list. We store the address, the confirmation status, and its
timestamps in our database, hosted in the EU (MongoDB Atlas, Frankfurt
region); emails are sent through **Resend** (Plus Five Five, Inc.,
2261 Market Street #5039, San Francisco, CA 94114, USA) as our
processor. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR). Every
digest email contains an unsubscribe link; unsubscribing is one click,
and your address is then deleted from the list.

## Contact form

If you write to us through the [contact form](/contact), we process the
data you enter — name, email address, and your message — to answer you.
Your message reaches us as an email, delivered through Resend to our
support inbox. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR where your message
concerns a contract or pre-contractual question, otherwise our
legitimate interest in answering (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). Messages are
retained as long as the conversation requires, then deleted.

## Recipients and transfers to third countries

The processors named above — Vercel, Sentry, and Resend — act on our
behalf under data processing agreements. Where processing touches the
United States, transfers rest on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework
(Vercel and Resend are certified participants) and, where applicable,
EU standard contractual clauses; Sentry keeps this site's error data in
the EU. We do not sell personal data, and no data is shared for
advertising.

## Storage duration

Server logs are kept for the hosting provider's technical retention
period, error events for 90 days, the digest address until you
unsubscribe, and contact messages for the duration of the conversation.
Beyond that, personal data is deleted when the purpose it was collected
for ends.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15),
- **rectification** of inaccurate data (Art. 16),
- **erasure** (Art. 17) and **restriction of processing** (Art. 18),
- **data portability** (Art. 20),
- **object** to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21),
- **withdraw consent** at any time with effect for the future
  (Art. 7 (3)) — for the digest, the unsubscribe link is exactly that.

To exercise any of these, one email to support@spiritsdontfly.com is
enough.
